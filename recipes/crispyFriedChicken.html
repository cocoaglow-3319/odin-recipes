Crispy Fried Chicken!

Description

This crispy fried chicken features a golden, crunchy crust on the outside and juicy, tender meat on the inside. Seasoned with simple spices and fried to perfection, it is a classic comfort food that everyone loves.

Serve this hot and crispy chicken with your favorite sides like mashed potatoes, coleslaw, or cornbread. It makes a wonderful family dinner for the weekend or any special gathering.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs chicken pieces
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • Vegetable oil for frying

Steps

  1. Soak the chicken pieces in buttermilk for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Mix the flour, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and black pepper in a large bowl.
  3. Take each piece of chicken from the buttermilk and dredge it thoroughly in the seasoned flour mixture.
  4. Heat the vegetable oil in a large deep skillet or Dutch oven to 350°F (175°C).
  5. Fry the chicken in batches for 12 to 15 minutes per batch until golden brown and cooked through.
  6. Transfer the fried chicken to a wire rack or paper towel-lined plate to drain before serving.
