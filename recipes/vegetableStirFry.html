Vegetable Stir-Fry!

Description

This colorful vegetable stir-fry is a quick and healthy meal packed with fresh, crisp vegetables. Tossed in a savory soy-garlic sauce, it brings out a vibrant flavor in every single bite.

Serve this warm dish over steamed white or brown rice. It makes a light and easy dinner for busy weeknights.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 sliced bell pepper
  • 1 sliced carrot
  • 1 cup snap peas
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 2 minced garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp grated ginger

Steps

  1. Heat the sesame oil in a large pan over medium-high heat.
  2. Add the minced garlic and grated ginger, cooking for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Add the carrots, broccoli, bell pepper, and snap peas to the pan.
  4. Stir-fry the vegetables for 5 to 7 minutes until tender yet crisp.
  5. Pour the soy sauce over the vegetables and toss well to coat evenly.
  6. Remove from heat and serve hot over rice.
